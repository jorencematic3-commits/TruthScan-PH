A simple habit

Pause. Check. Then decide.

Good verification takes more than a convincing headline. Start with these questions before you share.

01 / SOURCE

Who is behind it?

Look for the original publisher, author, date, and links to primary material.

02 / CONTEXT

What might be missing?

Read beyond the headline. Check whether a quote, image, or statistic has been taken out of context.

03 / EVIDENCE

Can it be corroborated?

Compare independent reporting and trace important claims back to their evidence.

A little curiosity can change what we pass along.
